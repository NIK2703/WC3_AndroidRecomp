## B. Split / merge / reorder / drop

**Split Step 2** into **2a baseline + fixtures** (no runtime changes; record artifacts, source hashes, import fixture) and **2b manifest/layout generation** (layout/corpus JSON, `guest_layout.h`, remove the `35` filter). The baseline must exist and be verified before any generated-header or build-script edit; the current step mixes both and can destroy the "before" state.

**Split Step 3** into **3a shared ABI + adapter**, **3b per-DLL migration**, **3c delete old tables**. Do not merge 403 entries in one diff. Keep the old tables as the oracle during migration and compare bindings at startup.

**Split Step 6** into **6a choose the canonical frontend and remove dead D3D8/legacy links**, **6b add the `d9vk` provider adapter and decide `native_vk` status**, **6c generator/spec checks**. The interface cannot be designed before deciding whether thin bridge or `hle_d3d9.cpp` is the 1.27 default.

**Split Step 9** into **9a documentation/policy truth** (move to Step 1/2) and **9b legacy deletion + end-to-end validation** (late). AGENTS/README must not remain wrong while Steps 2-8 run.

**Reorder**: extract `GuestRunner` and lifecycle state transitions (core of Step 7) right after Step 3, before Step 5. Step 5's pause/resume/surface-loss acceptance depends on it. Audio device-loss belongs in Step 5 (audio backend), not Step 7.

**Merge**: move CI skeleton, `check_arch.py`, `check_docs.py --strict`, and source-hash checks into Step 2; keep full build unification in Step 8. Move `check_layout`/`check_corpus` into Step 2 as planned.

**Drop**: `GfxProvider` at `IDirect3D9`; `win32_d3d9`; the new `gen_d3d9_spec.py`; the "PE supplies argc" claim. Do not drop `native_vk`; keep it as a fallback but not on the default path until frame/caps parity is proven.

## C. Three riskiest unchecked assumptions

1. **One renderer interface fits both DXVK and `native_vk`.** Verify cheaply: `python tools/gen_d3d9_thin_bridge.py --check`; `Select-String -Path runtime\host\hle_d3d9.cpp -Pattern 'd9vk_' | Measure-Object` (76 unique); inspect `runtime/host/hle_d3d9_vk.cpp:2554` for the C API; read `tools/android/build_boot_probe.ps1:90` for the thin default. If `native_vk` has no `IDirect3D9`, the interface is false.
2. **The HLE tables can be merged without ABI drift.** Verify cheaply: `python tools/android/audit_import_arity.py` (already reports 365/403, 0 stdcall, 16 cdecl, 104 missing). Re-run after every tranche; require 0 stdcall drift and unchanged boot markers.
3. **A meaningful baseline/E2E can run now.** Verify: `Get-Item build27\wc3_host27.exe` (0 bytes), `adb devices` (not installed), `Get-ChildItem refs -Recurse -Include war3.exe,game.dll` (empty). If all three are unavailable, the baseline must be source/artifact-only and E2E must be an explicit later gate, not a Step 2 promise.
